About Everyday Finds SA

Everyday Finds SA is a new online shopping project focused on useful products at sensible prices. We're starting small and checking suppliers carefully so we can share clear product, cost and delivery information before customers order.

We don't list unverified products or promise delivery dates we haven't confirmed.

How ordering will work

  • Ask us about a product and its current availability.
  • We'll confirm the full price, delivery charge and estimated delivery window.
  • You'll review the details before deciding whether to proceed.
  • We'll confirm your order and provide updates through the contact method agreed.

Delivery areas

We intend to serve customers in South Africa. Delivery availability depends on the product and supplier and will be confirmed before payment.

Delivery fees and times

The delivery fee and estimated delivery window will be provided to the customer before an order is accepted. We will not advertise a fixed fee or delivery promise until it has been verified with the supplier or courier.

Order processing and updates

Orders will only be confirmed after product availability, total cost, payment arrangements and delivery details have been agreed. Customers should keep their order confirmation for reference.

Delays or delivery problems

If a supplier or courier reports a delay or delivery problem, we will contact the customer using the details provided and explain the available next steps.

Returns, refunds & cancellations

Draft policy — finalise this before taking payments.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

Contact us as soon as reasonably possible with your order details and a description of the problem. We will explain the next steps and work to resolve the issue in line with applicable South African consumer law.

Change of mind

Returns for change of mind may depend on the circumstances and applicable law. Contact us before sending an item back so that return arrangements can be confirmed. Do not assume all items are automatically returnable or that all return costs are covered.

Refunds

Where a refund is due, we'll confirm the process and expected timing with you. Timing may depend on the payment provider and the circumstances of the return.

Order cancellations

Contact us promptly if you want to cancel. Whether an order can be cancelled may depend on whether it has already been placed with the supplier or dispatched, and on applicable law.

Consumer rights

This summary is not a replacement for applicable consumer law. Nothing in this policy is intended to limit rights that customers have under South African law.
